What to exclude

Do not chase unstitched fabric, men’s clothing, bridal lehengas, sarees, raw-silk suits, cheap/discount terms or knitted winter sets when the inventory does not match. The other “Rar Studio” in the screenshot is a different business; its categories should not be copied.

Google thumbnails and image rows

The screenshots show image-enhanced search results. They are not proof of ProductGroup variants: these five distinct designs should not be falsely grouped as colour variants of one product. Search relevance, indexing and Google’s image selection determine which non-brand searches can display them. There is no switch that forces an image row or visibility for every keyword.

Verified live gap: A Googlebot fetch of the homepage returned zero image tags. The local update now includes one product photo beside each product link. These are real images already visible on the shopper homepage.

Bandwidth: Crawler homepage photos use Cloudinary f_auto,q_auto:eco,c_limit,w_320,dpr_1. Five 320px thumbnails, fixed DPR, lazy loading, and the same URLs reused in catalogue structured data. Unsupported/non-Cloudinary URLs are skipped instead of exposing an uncompressed original. Existing product-page image and sitemap references were not increased. Those existing references still allow crawlers to discover larger product images; thumbnails alone do not cap all crawler traffic.

CollectionPage/ItemList markup describes the existing catalogue and image associations; it does not guarantee a special carousel. Large-image previews are permitted, not forced. Google’s preferred-image metadata and product markup remain separate from the display quality used by shoppers.
